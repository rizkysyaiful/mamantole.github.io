## Make the record as clear as the decision

Good implementation includes a plan for documentation. We help teams organise the records and workflows they will discuss with their accountants and auditors.

## What we work through

- Map the information your finance team needs from treasury operations.
- Define transaction records, supporting evidence, and document ownership.
- Plan reconciliation and internal review workflows.
- Prepare questions and handover materials for your appointed advisers.

## What you take away

A reporting workflow, a document checklist, and a handover brief for your accounting and audit team.

## Start with your context

We agree the scope and deliverables with you before work begins. Bring your current process, the people involved, and the questions you want to resolve.
