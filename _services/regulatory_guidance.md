## Bring the right questions to the right specialists

Each organisation has its own operating context. We help identify where legal, tax, and regulatory input is needed and organise the information your appointed advisers need to review.

## What we work through

- Map the entities, jurisdictions, and stakeholders in scope.
- Build a register of questions for qualified local advisers.
- Assign owners for gathering documents and recording advice.
- Include specialist review points in the implementation plan.

## What you take away

An adviser briefing pack, an open-questions register, and a coordination plan. Legal opinions and tax conclusions remain with your qualified advisers.

## Start with your context

We agree the scope and deliverables with you before work begins. Bring your current process, the people involved, and the questions you want to resolve.
