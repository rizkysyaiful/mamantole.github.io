## Start with a shared mandate

A treasury policy gives stakeholders a common reference for what the organisation is trying to achieve and who owns each decision. We help teams turn their discussions into a working document for internal review.

## What we work through

- Clarify the purpose and boundaries of the programme.
- Map decision owners, approval steps, and escalation routes.
- Document operating assumptions and review triggers.
- Prepare a policy draft and a list of open decisions.

## What you take away

A policy draft, a responsibility map, and a structured set of questions for your leadership team.

## Start with your context

We agree the scope and deliverables with you before work begins. Bring your current process, the people involved, and the questions you want to resolve.
