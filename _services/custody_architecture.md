## Design around the people who operate it

Custody is an organisational responsibility as well as a technical design problem. We help your team examine how access, approvals, recovery, and continuity fit into its operating model.

## What we work through

- Document custody requirements and the people involved.
- Compare proposed workflows with your internal approval process.
- Map access responsibilities, handovers, and recovery procedures.
- Prepare a controlled walkthrough and implementation checklist.

## What you take away

A custody design brief, an operational responsibility map, and a documented review checklist.

## Start with your context

We agree the scope and deliverables with you before work begins. Bring your current process, the people involved, and the questions you want to resolve.
