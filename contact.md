Whether you are introducing Bitcoin to your leadership team or exploring a personal learning programme, a clear brief helps us understand where to begin.

## What to bring to the conversation

- Your country (Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, India, or the Philippines), organisation and role.
- The questions your team is working through.
- Any existing policies or workflows you would like to discuss.
- Your preferred scope and timing.

## What happens next

Once you connect with the team, the first step is to understand your needs. Together, we can identify the relevant education or advisory scope and agree the next steps.

Please keep wallet credentials, private keys, and sensitive financial records out of an introductory enquiry.
