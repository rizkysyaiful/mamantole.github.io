Dimas is Bahtera's partner in Indonesia. He is a co-founder of [Bitcoin Indonesia](https://bitcoinindonesia.xyz/) and of Bitcoin House Bali, and hosts the Bitcoin Indonesia Podcast. Based in Bandung, he is a long-time Bitcoin educator and community builder.

With Bahtera he brings executive education and treasury policy discussions to Indonesian companies, and coordinates with local legal, tax and accounting advisers. You can follow his work on [X](https://x.com/Silatoshi_id).
